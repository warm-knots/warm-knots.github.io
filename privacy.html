Warm Knots

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Warm Knots is a small handmade crochet shop based in Tagum City, Davao del Norte, Philippines. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares with us your name, email address and profile picture. We never see or receive your Google password. If you use our website to save a cart or place an order, we also keep the items you chose.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and greet you by name.
  • To save your cart and your orders to your account.
  • To contact you about an order you placed.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where your information is stored

Your sign-in details and cart are stored in your own web browser on the device you use. Clearing your browser data removes them. If we later add online ordering, we will update this policy to describe where order information is kept.

Google user data

Warm Knots' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We only share information when it is needed to complete your order (for example, arranging a meetup or delivery) or when the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out at any time, and you can ask us to delete any information we hold about you. You can also remove Warm Knots' access in your Google Account under Security > Third-party apps & services. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you have the right to access, correct and request deletion of your personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
